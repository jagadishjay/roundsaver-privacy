RoundSaver Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

RoundSaver is a free Android app that shows where your money goes, rounds up your purchases, and reminds you to move the round-ups into your own savings account. This policy explains, in plain English, what the app does with your information.

The short version: RoundSaver has no internet access. Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone, and we (the developer) never receive any of your data.

What RoundSaver reads

With your permission (Android's SMS permission), RoundSaver reads SMS messages on your phone from two kinds of sender only:

  • Banks (sender IDs such as AD-HDFCBK, VM-SBIUPI): to find your purchases and money received — the amount, who you paid or who paid you, the time, and the last 4 digits of the account or card that your bank already shows in the message.
  • Shops' refund messages (sender IDs of shops such as Meesho, Amazon or Flipkart): only messages saying a refund was processed, so a refund shows under the shop's name. All other messages from these senders are ignored.

RoundSaver does not read or keep:

  • OTPs or verification codes (these are discarded immediately and never stored);
  • messages from people, offers or promotions, or any sender that is not a bank or a listed shop;
  • your contacts, call logs, location, photos, files, camera or microphone.

You can use RoundSaver without SMS access by adding purchases by hand.

What RoundSaver stores

Everything is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • your purchases, money received and refunds (amount, name, category, time, last 4 digits of the account or card), and the original bank or shop SMS text so you can check it;
  • your savings history (amounts, dates, whether each save went through) and your savings goal;
  • your settings: the UPI ID of your savings account, the name on that account as shown in your bank's SMS, round-up size, reminder day and time, language and theme.

Full account or card numbers are never stored. Your data is excluded from Android cloud backups.

Other things the app does on your phone

  • App icons: to show, for example, the Swiggy or Amazon icon next to a purchase, RoundSaver checks whether that shop's app is installed on your phone and shows its icon. This check happens on the phone only; no list of your apps is stored or sent anywhere.
  • Pay by QR: when you choose to save by QR code, RoundSaver creates a QR image of your own savings UPI ID and the amount, and saves it briefly in your phone's Pictures/RoundSaver folder so your UPI app can scan it. It is deleted again once the save is finished or cancelled.
  • Backups: if you make a backup, or turn on automatic backup, RoundSaver writes an encrypted backup file to a folder you choose on your phone. The password for automatic backups is kept in the Android Keystore on your phone. Backup files never leave your phone unless you move them yourself.
  • App lock: if you turn it on, RoundSaver uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock. RoundSaver never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Notifications and reminders: reminders and purchase alerts are created on the phone at the time you choose.

What RoundSaver shares

Nothing. RoundSaver does not have the Android internet permission, so it cannot upload, sync or send your data anywhere. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking.

When you save, you move the money yourself with a UPI app that you choose (for example Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM), by scanning the QR code or by a transfer to your own account. You approve the payment in that app with your UPI PIN. What that app does is covered by its own privacy policy.

RoundSaver never holds your money

RoundSaver is a tracking and reminder tool. It is not a bank, wallet or investment product and never holds, moves or has access to your money. Payments go directly from your bank account to your own savings account through your UPI app.

Your choices

  • Turn SMS access off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → RoundSaver → Permissions.
  • Export your data as a CSV file from Settings → Export data as CSV.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

RoundSaver is not directed at children under 18.
